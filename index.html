Ordering supplies, by phone, fax or email

Tell Hank what you need. He finds it and has it delivered to your door, without you going online.

  1. Tell Hank what you need: "a 50-foot rubber garden hose and a box of canning lids."
  2. Hank faxes or emails a page of choices with pictures, prices and delivery dates, or reads them to you on the phone.
  3. Check the one you want, write how many, and fax it back, or just call and say the number.
  4. Hank sends your order to look over: items, tax, shipping and the total.
  5. Nothing is ordered until you say YES with the code on your order page.
  6. It ships to your door. Hank lets you know when it ships, and you can call any time to ask where it is.
